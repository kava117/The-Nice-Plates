OMR TOOL COMPARISON: oemer, homr, Audiveris
============================================

oemer
-----
What it is: End-to-end deep-learning OMR (pure Python, pip install oemer).
Best input: Messy real-world images - phone photos, skewed or unevenly lit
  scans. Deskews images automatically. Works one page image at a time.
Struggles with: Dense polyphony, multiple voices on one staff, orchestral
  scores. Often misses ornaments, dynamics, and less common symbols. Slow on CPU.
Instruments: Best on single-staff melodic parts (flute, violin, voice, etc.)
  and simple piano (grand staff). Accuracy falls off as the number of staves
  per system grows.

homr
----
What it is: OMR built on oemer's segmentation plus a transformer model that
  reads each staff (Python, installed from the GitHub repo with Poetry).
Best input: Camera pictures of sheet music. Generally better pitch and rhythm
  accuracy than oemer.
Struggles with: Focuses on pitch and rhythm only - ignores dynamics,
  articulation, and other symbols (usually fine for MIDI). Research-grade:
  expect rough edges in setup and on unusual layouts.
Instruments: Same sweet spot as oemer - single-staff parts and piano grand
  staff. Not a good fit for large ensemble scores.

Audiveris
---------
What it is: Mature open-source OMR engine (Java). Also what MuseScore uses
  for PDF import.
Best input: Cleanly engraved scores - IMSLP scans, printed editions, PDFs
  exported from notation software. Handles multi-page PDFs and multi-part
  scores natively.
Recognizes: Much more notation than the other two - dynamics, articulations,
  sometimes lyrics.
Struggles with: Photos, handwritten music, low-resolution scans, skewed or
  curved pages. Wants flat scans at ~300 DPI. Heavier setup (Java app).
Instruments: Any instrument in standard staff notation, including piano,
  chamber, and full ensemble scores - multi-part layouts are its strength.

Shared limitations
------------------
- None handle handwritten music well.
- None read guitar/bass tablature; standard notation only.
- Percussion staves and unusual clefs are unreliable across all three.

Quick pick
----------
- Clean PDF (any instrumentation)  -> Audiveris
- Phone photo, solo part or piano  -> homr, fall back to oemer
- Large ensemble score             -> Audiveris only
