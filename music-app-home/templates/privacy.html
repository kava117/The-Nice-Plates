Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The Nice Plates helps you practice music from your own sheet music. This page says what we collect, why, and who else is involved. Plain language; not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

  • Account. If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account id, name and email so we can recognise you next time.
  • Your music. Pieces you upload (PDF, photo or MusicXML), and the MusicXML and MIDI we make from them so you can practise in the browser.
  • Practice record. Timer sessions, passages you mark, Yes / Not yet reps, Listen results shown on the score, and the playing levels you set in onboarding.
  • Recordings. Only if you press Record a take; we store that audio so you can play it back.
  • Coach messages. Questions you type to the coach, and its replies, for that conversation in your browser. The question is also sent to the AI provider so it can answer.
  • Technical. A signed session cookie to keep you logged in, and your theme choice in local storage on your device.

What stays on your device

Listen hears you through the microphone and runs a pitch model in your browser. That audio is not recorded and is not sent to our servers. Theme and tour “seen” flags also stay only on your device.

How we use it

  • To show your library, score, Progress charts and passage reps.
  • To run the practice timer and save sessions you stop.
  • To turn photos or PDFs of sheet music into a playable score (OMR), which may use our own tools, a separate reading service, or an AI vision model when needed.
  • To power the coach from your own practice record and the question you just asked.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your sheet music or practice data for advertising.

Who else may see data

  • Google — sign-in only; we receive your profile basics after you approve.
  • Our host and database (for example Render and Neon) — store the app and the data above so the site can run.
  • Anthropic — when you use the coach, your question and a summary of your practice record are sent so Claude can answer. Do not put secrets in coach messages.
  • OpenAI — only if a recording-transcription feature is switched on with an API key; otherwise unused.
  • Sheet-reading service — if configured, photo or PDF pages may be sent there to be read into MusicXML.

Guests

Without signing in, everyone shares one demo account. Anything a guest uploads or practices can be seen by other guests. Sign in with Google for a private library and practice record.

How long we keep it

We keep account and practice data while your account exists and the project is running. Free hosting may wipe a local database on restart if no external database is configured; with a configured database, data persists across deploys. You can sign out at any time. To delete an account and its data, contact us (see below).

Children

The site is aimed at people learning an instrument, including students. If you are under the age where you can agree to online services in your country, ask a parent or guardian before signing in. We do not knowingly collect more than needed to run practice features.

Security

We use HTTPS on the live site, signed session cookies, and access controls so one signed-in user’s pieces are not served as another’s. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Your choices

  • Sign out from Settings.
  • Skip the coach; it is optional and needs an API key on the server.
  • Do not use Record a take if you do not want audio stored.
  • Use Listen knowing processing stays in the browser.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact details for the course or team running this project, or open an issue on the project’s GitHub repository.

Also see our Terms of Use.
